# Community Code of Conduct

OrchidOS aims to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive research community.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful and patient.
- Discuss technical ideas, not personal attributes.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar concepts without ridicule.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Credit other people's work.
- Be honest about uncertainty, limitations, and project status.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Do not post private personal information in public issues.

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove content that violates this code and may restrict participation when necessary.
